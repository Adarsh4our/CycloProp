# Four planar articulated pushrod prototypes

This revision applies the first planar-link prototype to all four blade stations. The user's three additional offset arms are reused, with their journal, drive-flat and axial-seat mates retained. Their copied tube/socket set is reused for the second linkage; its earlier socket-end coincidence constraints are superseded to obtain95mm between ball centres. The remaining links use the same saved part definitions. No new blade or airfoil geometry is introduced.

All stations use the offset22.075mm arm,12mm effective arm radius,95mm ball-centre distance and85mm tube. Each has two separately moving ball inserts, two socket housings, spacers, washers and mounting screws. Their dimensions and manufacturing limitations are described in PLANAR_REVISION.md. Socket assembly/pullout retention, materials, fits, adhesive strength, arm bending/fatigue, threaded-joint strength and locking remain unverified.

The Assem3 instance was found set toRigid. It is now set toFlexible in Assem20 so the outer control ring can rotate relative to its carrier. The original supplied bearing internals are not redesigned. Approximately4.1degrees of ring indexing adjustment is used for the provisional assembly pose, bringing the fitted blade pitch values within35degrees at the user's current carriage setting. This is a setup choice, not a verified neutral-pitch calibration or cyclic-pitch law.

Temporary rotor-angle, ring-angle and carriage setup constraints are used while fitting and checking, then the setup angle mates are suppressed and the carriage limits restored. No physical coupling defining ring-to-rotor timing has been designed. The four links and a freely rotating ring leave a phase degree of freedom; a saved pose does not resolve that mechanical requirement.

Full rotor rotation remains unverified and obstructed by the existing frame/support arrangement. A check of the current pose or a small carriage movement does not establish360degree clearance or the required cyclic-pitch amplitude. Do not fabricate this module from these prototype models without resolving the frame sweep, phase drive, blade bushings/retention and production definitions.

FOUR_LINKS_VALIDATION.json records the final component mapping, native mate and geometry checks, exclusions, original carriage offset, source hashes and pre-change backup. Earlier single-link validation records apply only to their recorded revisions.

The copied socket constraints are replaced with explicit tube-relative plane constraints: each socket centre is 47.5 mm from the tube midpoint. Both spherical joints remain articulated. During fitting, the first linkage selected the alternative assembly branch (about 105 degrees pitch); it was returned to the intended branch (about 30.6 degrees at the setup pose). This is another reason that mate validity alone does not establish the intended motion.

Subsequent assembly cleanup removed 31 obsolete suppressed component instances and 144 suppressed mates, including the temporary setup-angle mates. Source part files were retained. The assembly rebuilt successfully with all 276 active component instances retained, no significant transform changes, no active mate errors and no suppressed root-level components remaining. ASSEMBLY_CLEANUP.json records the saved assembly hash and backup. The earlier geometry/motion results remain tied to their original file hashes; no new motion or interference sweep was performed for this cleanup.

Final focused checks passed at the saved position, X +0.5 mm, and after return: all four ball-centre distances were 95 mm, joint centres aligned, axial offsets negligible, blade pitch within +/-35 degrees, and no active affected-mate errors. The saved-position solid check found no intersections involving the revised components and the listed surrounding components (115 body templates). Interference was not checked at the displaced position or across a motion sweep. The original carriage offset, approximately X +0.775 mm / Y +6.241 mm, was restored. The JSON report lists the exact scope and exclusions.

For current-position clearance, Frame_Mounting_RevA has an underside relief outside a protected 28 mm central radius. The relieved underside is 39.5 mm above the fixed-base underside, leaving 9.5 mm at the support feet. Four replacement standoffs are 36.5 mm long, OD6/ID3.4, at X = +/-24 mm and Y = +/-70 mm. The fixed base has additional support extensions and matching holes; frame fasteners move to those locations. The existing M3x55 fasteners retain their axial positions. Earlier support lengths and Y = +/-52 mm locations are superseded for this assembly. The original frame part is retained. These changes require material, stiffness, strength and manufacturing review; they do not resolve the full rotor sweep.
